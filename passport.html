Your guide, before you apply

Before you begin

A fresh passport is for someone who is applying for an ordinary passport for the first time. Use only the official Passport Seva portal for registration, application, payment and appointment booking.

Documents to keep ready

The exact list can differ by applicant category. Use the official Document Advisor before submitting your application.

  • Proof of present address
  • Proof of date of birth
  • Original documents and one set of self-attested photocopies for your appointment
Check official Document Advisor ↗

Fee

From ₹2,500

For a 36-page, 10-year ordinary passport. The correct fee depends on your category and application type; check the official calculator before payment.

Calculate official fee ↗

How to apply

  1. 1

    Register on Passport Seva

    Create an account with your email address on the official Passport Seva portal, then sign in.

    Open Passport Seva portal ↗
  2. 2

    Fill the fresh passport application

    From the Services section, choose “Apply for Fresh Passport/Re-issue of Passport” and complete the online form carefully.

  3. 3

    Pay and book an appointment

    Pay the applicable fee online and choose a Passport Seva Kendra or Post Office Passport Seva Kendra appointment.

    Read official application steps ↗
  4. 4

    Visit the selected centre

    Carry your appointment confirmation and required original documents with self-attested photocopies.

  5. 5

    Track your application

    Use the official Passport Seva portal to check application status after submission.

    Go to Passport Seva ↗

Ready to start?

Use the official government portal to register and apply. Never share your portal password with anyone.
