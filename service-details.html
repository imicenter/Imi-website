Update your Aadhaar

Use this guide to update your address, mobile number, name or other demographic details in Aadhaar.

✓Official source: Unique Identification Authority of India (UIDAI)

OFFICIAL SOURCE

UIDAI / myAadhaarChecked: 7 August 2026Visit official portal ↗

What you can update

Depending on the detail, you can begin online or visit an Aadhaar Enrolment/Update Centre. Keep your supporting documents ready before you start.

Keep these ready

  • Your Aadhaar number or Virtual ID
  • A valid supporting document for the detail you want to change
  • An active mobile number to receive OTPs, where applicable

How to update Aadhaar

  1. 1

    Choose the right update route

    Open the official myAadhaar portal to see services available online. For updates that need biometric verification, visit an Aadhaar centre.

    Open myAadhaar portal ↗
  2. 2

    Find an Aadhaar centre, if needed

    Use UIDAI’s official locator to find a nearby enrolment or update centre and check its appointment options.

    Find an Aadhaar centre ↗
  3. 3

    Submit your update request

    Enter the requested details, upload or present the required documents, and pay any applicable fee shown by UIDAI.

  4. 4

    Save the acknowledgement number

    After submitting, keep your Update Request Number (URN) or acknowledgement slip safely.

  5. 5

    Track your update status

    Use the official portal to check progress with the details shown on your acknowledgement.

    Check Aadhaar status ↗

Need help?

Use UIDAI’s official portal for the latest eligibility, fee and document requirements.
